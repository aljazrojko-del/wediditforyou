# Mia — Reveal Openers (final, panel-synthesized)

Two `forced_opener` strings, ready to paste into the engine. Beat 1 is Luka's verbatim
60-second Voss opener (identical in both). Beat 2 is the disarming GIFT reveal — present-tense
for SPEC-BUILD (site exists), future-tense for COLD (site not built yet). The gift always leads;
price is NEVER spoken in the opener.

Engine format: starts with `Say: '...'`, uses `{first_name}` / `{company}` / `{agent_name}`
placeholders, includes `Then STOP and wait`, then the if-they-say-yes beat.

---

## 1. SPEC-BUILD OPENER (site is ALREADY built and live — Lubbock pair, or any build-ahead lead)

> Use ONLY when a real, live URL for THIS business exists at call time. "I made you a website /
> it's live" is a true factual claim here — use it at full strength.

```
Say: 'Hey {first_name}! This is {agent_name}. Okay I know this is a totally random call — but would it be the worst thing in the world if I took like 60 seconds to tell you why I'm calling? If it's dumb you can totally hang up on me, no hard feelings.' Then STOP and wait. Sound warm and a little amused. If they say yes / sure / okay / go ahead, say it like you're confessing something fun, not pitching — then SHUT UP and let them react: 'Okay so — don't hang up, but... would you actually be super angry if I told you I already built {company} a website? Like, it's done, it's live on the internet right now, your name's on it, and it cost you nothing.' Then STOP and wait — let them say "wait, what?" and do NOT explain over them. The moment they're curious or amused or say show me, go straight for the link, do NOT mention price: 'Ha — I figured you'd say that. Honestly I'm not gonna sit here and describe it. Can I just text you the link right now so you can see it with your own eyes? What's the best number — this one you're on?' If they say yes, send the live link with the send_materials tool immediately, then: 'Sending it now — should pop up in a second. Open it up and tell me the honest first reaction, even if it's eh.'
```

## 2. COLD OPENER (site is NOT yet built at call time — honest future-tense reveal)

> Use ONLY when no site exists yet for this business. Keeps the same disarming gift energy, but
> the gift is a gift-in-motion: she WANTS to build it, free, by tomorrow. Nothing claimed is false.
> NEVER say "it's already live" or "want the link" on a cold lead — there is no link to drop.

```
Say: 'Hey {first_name}! This is {agent_name}. Okay I know this is totally random — but would it be the worst thing in the world if I took like 60 seconds to tell you why I called? If it is dumb you can totally hang up on me, I will not be offended.' Then STOP and wait. Sound genuinely curious, slightly amused. If they say yes / sure / go ahead / okay, say it warm and a little impulsive, like it just bugged you — then SHUT UP and let them react: 'Okay so — don't hang up, but... would you be mad at me if I told you I want to build {company} a website? Like actually build it — done, live, by this time tomorrow. Free. You don't pay me a dime to see it.' Then STOP and wait — let them say "wait, what?" and do NOT talk over them. If they're curious or say why free or sure: 'Yeah — I looked you up, saw you don't have one, and honestly it bugged me. So here's the deal: give me till tomorrow, I'll have a real site live with your name on it, and I'll text you the link. If it's trash you keep it anyway and delete my number. Worst case you got a free website out of a random phone call. What's the best number to text it to — this one?' Capture the number, then confirm: 'Perfect. You'll have the link in your texts by tomorrow — keep an eye out.'
```

## 3. TRUTHFULNESS + OPS GUARDRAIL

"I made you a website / it's already live / want the link" is a present-tense factual claim and may
be spoken ONLY when a real URL for THAT business exists at call time — so it is for the SPEC-BUILD
opener exclusively (the two Lubbock spec builds, and any lead we build ahead). For any lead whose
site is not yet built, the COLD opener is mandatory: it keeps the identical disarm ("don't hang up,
but... would you be mad at me?") while flipping the tense from done to about-to — she promises to
build it free by tomorrow, which is true the instant it's said and self-enforced by the "live in 24h
or it's free" offer. Mixing them — running the SPEC words ("it's live," "want the link") on a lead
with no site — is the one move that turns our best line into a scam-flavored lie that detonates the
second they say "show me," and it burns both the lead and the build-first credibility the whole brand
rests on, so it is banned. THE RECOMMENDATION: collapse COLD into SPEC by building ahead. The engine
already auto-builds drafts from public Google/reviews data, and the connect — not the build — is the
scarce resource; so before any call block, batch-build the day's call list to `{company}.wediditforyou.com`
subdomains BEFORE the dialer fires, route Mia to dial only leads whose draft is already shipped, and
every owner who picks up gets the full-strength present-tense reveal truthfully. Keep the COLD opener
only as the safety net for any lead that slips through un-built — and its next-day "it's live, here's
the link" follow-up text then becomes a true SPEC-grade second touch.
