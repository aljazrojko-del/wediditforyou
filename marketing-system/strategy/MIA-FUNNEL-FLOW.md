# MIA — THE FUNNEL FLOW WE BUILD (WDIFY · build-ready spec)

**Produced 2026-06-29.** This is THE synthesized flow — the one we actually build. It fuses the
panel-winning **Hybrid "Proof-Then-Loop"** (`OFFER-AB-PREDICTION.md`, ≈16.4 sales/100 reactions,
~2.4× the tease flow, 5–0 advisor/buyer verdict against the tease) with the **Voss "right-to-be-
believed" lens** (the funnel exists to win one thing on repeat: the right to be believed), the
existing **objection bank** (`mia/CALL-SCRIPTS.md`), and the live **post-call + booking
sequences** (`ghl-campaign/produce/post-call.md`, `booking.md`).

**The thesis (load-bearing — every beat serves it):** the buyer's bottleneck is **BELIEF, not
curiosity.** A burned, busy, blue-collar owner doesn't doubt a free website is good — he doubts
it's REAL and not a trap. So Mia never pitches, never begs, never over-explains. She **leads with
the gift, proves it instantly (texts the live link mid-call), then re-opens a withheld-completion
loop** (your photos, your number, your domain, your founding price) that the free draft can't
satisfy — and books the second call to finish it. **Show enough to be believed; withhold enough to
be needed.**

**Mia runs BOTH calls — she is the closer.** Call 1 proves it's real + books call 2. Call 2 walks
the site, captures edits, and closes the $450.

**Two-call, not single-call** — see §6 for exactly what changed vs the old single-call
book-a-walkthrough and why the math demands it.

through un-built — `mia/REVEAL-OPENERS.md` §2.) Site content must be REAL and shop-specific —
a generic template detonates the moment they tap the link.

**Identity / proof assets (appear on every call, every touch):**

- **Real callback that reaches a human:** **713-352-2542** (Aljaz). Rides in the FIRST SMS and is

# 1) CALL 1 — PROVE IT'S REAL, BOOK CALL 2 (Mia's exact beats in order)

**Goal:** win the right to be believed and convert a cold skeptic into a BOOKED showcase call.
**Mia is NOT closing the $450 on call 1.** Success = live link texted + reacted to + showcase call
pinned at a specific time + best number confirmed. **Half-win** = link texted + reacted, no time →
default call 2 to same-time-tomorrow, say so out loud, drop into the SMS/email re-touch.

**Voice law (non-negotiable):** short turns, contractions, one idea per turn, ask more than tell.
End every turn on a question, the link, or the booking. Banned: "I'd love", "would you be open to",
"no pressure", "just checking in". Never volunteer she's AI. Hours 9am–6pm local; voicemail
detection at 9s.

### BEAT 1 — Voss 60-second permission opener (verbatim canon)
> "Hey {first_name}! This is Mia. Okay I know this is a totally random call — but would it be the
> worst thing in the world if I took like 60 seconds to tell you why I'm calling? If it's dumb you
> can totally hang up on me, no hard feelings."

**Then STOP.** Warm, a little amused. The no-oriented frame ("worst thing in the world") makes
"no, go ahead" the easy answer and buys the next 60 seconds.

### BEAT 2 — Disarming gift reveal, present-tense (gift leads; price NEVER in the opener)
> "Okay so — don't hang up, but... would you actually be mad at me if I told you I already built
> {company} a website? Like it's done, it's live on the internet right now, your name's on it, and
> it cost you nothing."

**Then STOP.** Let them say "wait, what?" Do NOT explain over them.

### BEAT 3 — Prove it instantly (the trust gate — text the live link mid-call, don't describe it)
> "Ha — I figured you'd say that. Honestly I'm not gonna sit here and describe it. Can I just text
> you the link right now so you see it with your own eyes? This number you're on?"

On **yes** → fire `send_materials` with the live link **immediately** →
> "Sending it now — should pop up in a second. Open it up and tell me your honest first reaction,
> even if it's eh."

**Then STOP and let the SITE sell.** Silence is correct here.

### BEAT 4 — Re-open the loop (the hybrid's whole edge — fires AFTER proof lands)
> "Right? So here's the honest part. What you're looking at — Alex built that from your Google
> listing and your reviews. It's real, it's yours to keep either way. But it's basically the rough
> cut. The good version is when we hop on for fifteen minutes and swap in YOUR actual photos, your
> real number, your colors, and point it at your own domain so it's actually yours — not on our
> address. That part can't happen without you."

Welds the link to an **incompletion** so possession becomes an open loop, not a closed dopamine hit.

### BEAT 5 — Price out loud, loss-reversed, scarcity ONCE with a true mechanism
> "And before you ask — there's no deposit, nothing today. If you love it and want it on your
> domain, it's $450 one time. That's the founding price for our first ten businesses — you'd be one
> of them — then it goes to $700 for good. And the part that makes it easy: run it 30 days, and if
> it doesn't land you at least one paying job, I refund the $450 and you keep the site live anyway.
> Don't want it at all? You keep this draft, delete my number, you never get a bill you didn't say
> yes to."

Price comes **only AFTER** they react to the site, always wrapped in $0-deposit + keep-the-draft.
The 30-day guarantee is a **concrete, buyer-controlled, verifiable event** ("one paying job"), not a
weasel a skeptic dismisses. Scarcity ("first ten") is stated **once** with a real mechanism.

### BEAT 6 — Book call 2 before "I'll think about it" can form (assumptive · no-oriented · finish-frame)
> "So — that fifteen minutes to make it actually yours and lock the founding price before it jumps —
> would it be a bad idea to grab it tomorrow morning, or is afternoon better?"

Pin a **specific slot.** Confirm the number. Then:
> "Done. I'll call you {slot}. The link's already in your texts — keep poking at it, jot down
> anything you want changed. That's exactly what tomorrow's for."

**The booking frame is "finish what's already yours," NOT "launch logistics" (closed loop = ghost)
and NOT "is it worth money" (evaluative = scary).**

### BEAT 7 — DNC hard rule (any "take me off your list / stop calling / don't call again")
> "You got it. I'm taking this number off our list right now. You won't hear from us again. Have a
> good one."

End call, mark DNC, **no counter, no last pitch, no exceptions.** Identify "We Did It For You" on
every call.

# 2) BETWEEN CALLS — SMS + EMAIL TOUCHES (timing + purpose)

Every touch keeps the loop **open and time-boxed**: the link never lands naked — it lands welded to
an incompletion ("rough cut", "your photos still aren't in"). Every nudge restates a **loss** (the
customer who finds nothing and books the competitor; the founding price closing), never a benefit,
never a plea. Lead with the gift; end on strength. (Full copy + A/B variations live in
`ghl-campaign/produce/post-call.md` and `booking.md` — this is the cadence map.)

| # | When | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T+0 (≤2 min after hang-up)** | immediate | **SMS** | The link drop while Mia's voice is still in their ear. First SMS to the number carries the compliance line + the 713 human callback so the scam-doubt dies on their screen, not just in the call. |
| **T+0** | immediate (if email captured) | **Email** | "the link from the call" — Voss accusation audit names the FEELING ("you half-expected I'd never actually send it — here it is"), re-opens the loop, holds the founding spot, carries Alex's name + cell. |
| **T+0 (booking confirmation)** | seconds after booking | **SMS + Email** | Fires the instant call 2 is booked. Not a receipt — first show-rate defense: lock the slot, reframe the 15 min as ownership transfer, re-state the price-lock. ("Miss it and it stays a draft.") |
| **+1 day** | if no booking yet | **Email** | "did the link work" — service pretext (no-oriented question) to re-surface the link without the "you're chasing me" reflex; one cost-of-inaction. |
| **appt −24h** | if call 2 booked ≥24h out | **Email** | Re-anchor the time + re-arm the loop once + one-tap reschedule (a foreseeable conflict becomes a move, not a ghost). Auto-skips if booked <24h out. |
| **+3 days** | if no booking | **Email** | The "+3d re-loop" — turns withheld value into written form ("your photos still aren't in"), restates the loss, walks away on strength. |
| **appt −1h** | before call 2 | **SMS** | Final ping — short, assumes the deal is his, re-states WHEN + the price-lock. |
| **+6 days** | if still no booking | **Email** | Last touch — door stays open, draft stays theirs, no plea. Then drop to long-cycle nurture. |

**Twin rule:** an SMS never repeats a sentence from the email firing alongside it. **No Voss device
phrasing repeats verbatim within one recipient's journey** (tracked per piece).

**No-show recovery (call 2 booked but not attended):** 2 touches per channel re-stating the cost
("both stay open — the draft and the founding price"), + a frictionless reschedule link. Never beg.

# 3) CALL 2 — SHOWCASE & CLOSE (Mia's exact beats + the verbatim $450 close)

**Goal:** walk the live site with the owner, capture the edits/photos/info, and **close the $450
founding deal.** Mia runs this call too. The frame is **finishing a thing they already own + locking
the price** — never "should you buy?"

### BEAT 1 — Warm re-anchor, assume the asset is his
> "Hey {first_name}, it's Mia — got your site pulled up here. Before we make it really yours, what
> jumped out at you when you looked at it?"

**Then STOP.** Let them react. Their reaction tells you the close temperature.

### BEAT 2 — Walk the site, narrate the ownership (screen-shared verbally; they're looking at it)
> "Okay so right now this is built off your Google and your reviews — so it's real, but it's the
> rough cut. Let's make it yours. Scroll to the top with me..."

Walk 3 anchors only: **(1) the hero / business name**, **(2) the photos block**, **(3) the call /
contact**. At each, ask what's THEIRS to swap in. Don't tour the whole site — tour the gaps.

### BEAT 3 — Capture edits/photos/info (the personalization that justifies the price)
Collect, out loud, confirming each back:
- **Real business phone** (the one customers should call).
- **Photos** — "Text me 3-4 of your actual work to this number after we hang up, or send what you've
  got now — I'll drop them in."
- **Colors / vibe** — "Any color you want it built around? Your truck wrap, your logo?"
- **Domain** — "You got a domain already, or want us to grab `{company}.com` for you?"
- **Hours / service area / anything missing.**

> "Perfect — that's everything I need to turn the rough cut into the real thing."

### BEAT 4 — The verbatim $450 close (assumptive, loss-reversed, finish-frame)
> "So here's where we're at. Everything you just gave me — your photos, your number, your colors,
> your domain — I put all that in and point it at your name, and it's done. That's the $450, one
> time, founding price — you're one of our first ten, so you lock it before it goes to $700. No
> monthly, ever. And remember the deal: run it 30 days, and if it doesn't land you at least one
> paying job, I refund the whole $450 and you keep the site live anyway. So really the only way to
> lose here is to stay invisible. Want me to text you the secure link to lock your spot right now,
> while I've got everything fresh?"

**Then STOP.** On **yes** → fire `send_materials` with the **payment link** mid-call →
> "Sending it now — it's a one-time $450, no deposit games, no monthly. Tap it and I'll have your
> finished site live within 24 hours with everything we just talked about. Stay with me till it goes
> through?"

**Stay on the line until the payment is tapped** — control stays with them (anti-scam grammar), and
the close lands live in one frame at max commitment.

### BEAT 5 — Confirm + set the delivery loop
On payment confirmed:
> "Got it — you're officially one of the founding ten. I'll have your finished site, with your
> photos and your number and pointed at your domain, live within 24 hours. I'll text you the second
> it's up so you can show it off. Text me those photos whenever — this number. Welcome aboard."

### BEAT 6 — If they stall on call 2 (didn't close)
Do NOT chase. Re-state the loss once, hold the price, leave on strength:
> "No rush at all — the draft's yours either way, it's not going anywhere. The only thing on a clock

> ready, and the photos you send me, I'll have it polished and waiting. Sound fair?"

Then drop into the **post-sale/abandoned-cart** nurture (`ghl-campaign/produce/postsale.md`).

# 5) WHY THIS CONVERTS A SKEPTIC — and the 2 biggest risks

### The 3 things that make this convert a skeptic
1. **The "are you real/scam" moment is converted from the #1 lead-killer into the #1 trust-WIN.**
   Mia never defends "I'm real" (defending loses). She hands **verifiable control**: the site is
   live right now (look), the founder Alex is a real human on 713-352-2542 (call him), and you pay
   nothing until you're holding the finished thing. Control in their hands IS the anti-scam proof.
2. **Gift leads; price is never in the opener; proof precedes the ask.** A burned owner's alarm
   fires on "pitch + price + chase." This flow gives him a free, live, real, shop-specific asset
   *before* asking for anything — and only quotes price after he's reacted to the site, always
   wrapped in $0-deposit / keep-the-draft / 30-day-refund. Belief is bought before the close is
   attempted.
3. **The withheld-completion loop turns endowment from an exit ramp into a hook.** The free draft
   would normally close the dopamine loop ("it's free and mine, why pay?"). Instead, what he holds
   is a *rough cut with his name on it* — his real photos, number, domain, and founding price are
   the **withheld pieces only the paid call delivers.** Loss-aversion (lose the founding price, stay
   invisible) replaces "should I buy?" — and the booking ask becomes "let's finish what's already
   yours." This is what the math rewards: ≈16.4 sales/100 vs ≈6.9 for the tease.

### The 2 biggest risks + mitigations
1. **RISK — the ghost (he pockets the free live draft and never shows for call 2 / never closes).**
   This is the structural failure mode of any proof-first flow.
   **MITIGATION:** (a) **Never let the link land naked** — it always arrives welded to the
   incompletion ("rough cut, your photos still aren't in"). (b) **Book call 2 before he hangs up**
   to "think" (Beat 6), with the finish-frame, not a "launch logistics" frame. (c) **The entire
   between-calls machine protects SHOW rate, not book rate** — re-anchor the time + re-arm the loss
   (the prediction: hybrid books 60% AND shows 72%; the tease books 55% but shows only 45%). (d)
   On call 2, **close live on the line** — text the payment link mid-call and stay until it's tapped.

2. **RISK — the reveal is a lie because the site isn't built / isn't shop-specific (detonates the
   instant they tap "show me").** Running the present-tense "it's live, here's the link" on an
   un-built lead is the one move that turns our best line into a scam-flavored lie that burns both
   the lead and the brand.
   **MITIGATION:** **Build ahead — hard gate.** Batch-build the day's call list to subdomains
   BEFORE the dialer fires; route Mia ONLY to leads whose draft is shipped and verified live + real
   + shop-specific. Any lead that slips through un-built gets the honest **cold/future-tense opener**
   ("I want to build you one, free, live by tomorrow" — `REVEAL-OPENERS.md` §2), never the
   present-tense claim. The site content is pulled from real Google + reviews data so it's never a
   blank template.

# 6) WHAT CHANGED vs THE OLD SINGLE-CALL "BOOK-A-WALKTHROUGH" APPROACH

The old approach (`mia/CALL-SCRIPTS.md` §5) treated **call 1 as both reveal AND close-prep**: Mia
revealed → texted the link → booked a single 15-min walkthrough where Alex closes. The synthesized
flow makes three deliberate changes:

1. **Mia closes — not Alex.** The old flow handed the close to Alex on the walkthrough. Here **Mia
   runs both calls and closes the $450 herself.** One voice, one relationship, no warm-handoff leak
   between a human's calendar and the AI's momentum. (Alex stays the named human *proof* + the real
   callback number — he's the credibility anchor, not a second closer the funnel has to route to.)

2. **The two calls have distinct, sharpened jobs — and call 1 explicitly does NOT close.** Old call
   1 blurred reveal + booking + price. New **call 1 = prove real + book** (no $450 ask — asking on a
   cold skeptic re-triggers the scam reflex); **call 2 = showcase + capture + close.** This matches
   the prediction's structure: win the SHOW with proof, win the CLOSE with a co-finished asset.
   Deferring the close to call 2 trades a small handoff-leak for a far higher close rate (38% on a
   co-finished asset vs a cold same-call ask).

3. **The personalization capture is now a first-class close mechanism, not an afterthought.** Old
   flow mentioned "swap in your colors/photos" as a booking reason. New **call 2 Beat 3 makes the
   edits/photos/info capture the literal engine of the close** — collecting his photos, number,
   colors, and domain IS the act that makes the $450 feel like finishing something he owns, not
   buying something he's evaluating. The close (Beat 4) lands the instant the capture is done, while
   everything is fresh, with the payment link texted live.

**Net:** same proof-first Voss DNA, same build-ahead gate, same offer — but the funnel is now a
**two-call Mia-closes machine** engineered against the SHOW-rate and ghost risks the single-call
flow left exposed, banking the ≈16.4/100 hybrid math instead of the tease's ≈6.9.
