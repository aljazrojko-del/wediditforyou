# WDIFY OFFER A/B PREDICTION — Which Mia pitch closes more $450 sales?

**Date:** 2026-06-13 · **Panel head verdict** · Source of truth: `CONTEXT.md`, `mia/CALL-SCRIPTS.md`, `mia/REVEAL-OPENERS.md`

> **The question:** Of two pitches — **A: tease + book-to-reveal** (withhold the link, curiosity drives the booking) vs **B: preview-now + book-to-launch** (text the live link mid-call) — which converts more across the FULL funnel: connect → positive reaction → booked → SHOWS → closes $450?

---

## 1) THE PREDICTION

**Winner: HYBRID — "Prove it's real, then re-open the loop." (a.k.a. Proof-Then-Loop)**
**Confidence: 0.75**

**The single decisive reason:** This buyer's bottleneck is **skepticism, not curiosity.** A busy, burned, non-tech mobile-service owner does not lack interest in a free website — he lacks *belief that it's real and not a trap.* For him, "I built you a site but you have to book a call to see it" (Option A) **pattern-matches to the exact con grammar** — dangle, make him chase, corner him later. Withholding the link doesn't build anticipation in a skeptic; it **confirms the scam hypothesis**, so A leaks catastrophically at connect and no-show. Option B kills the scam fear in 10 seconds (its only real job) but then **spends its entire leverage for free** and reframes the call as committal "launch on your domain," inviting the polite "thanks, I'll think about it" ghost.

The winner takes **B's instant proof** (the only thing that buys trust with a skeptic) and immediately **re-arms A's open loop** — but the loop is NOT "come see the mystery." It's a *withheld piece of value the free draft can't satisfy*: **the 15-minute call is where Alex swaps in YOUR real photos, YOUR phone number, YOUR colors, points it at YOUR domain, and locks the $450 founding price before it jumps to $700.** Right now they hold a *shell with their name on it* — a taste, not the finished thing. That converts endowment from an exit ramp into a hook (Zeigarnik on a half-finished possession beats Zeigarnik on an unseen mystery), and converts the booking ask from "should you buy?" into "let's finish what's already yours."

> **Prove real. Don't reveal value. Show enough to be believed, withhold enough to be needed.**

**Load-bearing operational fact (this is what makes the hybrid possible):** On the live list, **740 of 742 leads have NO site at call time.** Pure-B is literally fictional for them — you can't text a link that doesn't exist, and faking it ("it's live, want the link?") on an un-built lead is the banned scam-flavored lie that detonates the instant they say "show me." **So the real decision isn't tease-vs-reveal — it's BUILD AHEAD.** Batch-build each day's call list to `{company}.wediditforyou.com` subdomains BEFORE the dialer fires (the engine already auto-drafts from public Google/reviews data — the *connect* is the scarce resource, not the build). Every owner who picks up then gets the full-strength hybrid truthfully. The two Lubbock spec-builds are already live → they get the FIRST calls at full strength today.

## 4) THE OPTIMAL MIA SCRIPT (Hybrid — exact words)

> **Link decision: SENT LIVE, mid-call** (not withheld). The link is the proof that earns the right to be trusted. **Re-open a NEW loop the instant it lands.** Booking is framed as **finishing/personalizing + locking the founding price** — NOT "launch logistics" (closed loop = ghost) and NOT "is it worth money" (evaluative = scary). Price is spoken OUT LOUD on this call (the skeptics demand it) but only AFTER they've reacted to the site, and always wrapped in $0-deposit / keep-the-draft loss-reversal.
>
> **Prerequisite: the site must be BUILT before the dial.** Batch-build the day's list to subdomains first; route Mia only to leads whose draft is shipped. Lubbock pair = live now → call first.

### Beat 1 — Voss 60-second opener (verbatim, identical to existing doctrine)
> "Hey {first_name}! This is {agent_name}. Okay I know this is a totally random call — but would it be the worst thing in the world if I took like 60 seconds to tell you why I'm calling? If it's dumb you can totally hang up on me, no hard feelings."
> *(STOP. Wait. Warm, a little amused.)*

### Beat 2 — Disarming gift reveal (present-tense, site IS built)
> "Okay so — don't hang up, but… would you actually be mad if I told you I already built {company} a website? Like, it's done, it's live on the internet right now, your name's on it, and it cost you nothing."
> *(STOP. Let them say "wait, what?" Do NOT explain over them.)*

### Beat 3 — PROVE IT (text the link immediately — this is B's move, the trust gate)
> "Ha — I figured you'd say that. I'm not gonna sit here and describe it. Let me just text you the link right now so you see it with your own eyes — this number you're on?"
> *(On yes → fire `send_materials` with the live link immediately.)*
> "Sending it now — should pop up in a sec. Open it up and tell me the honest first reaction, even if it's eh."
> *(STOP. Let them look. Let them react. Silence is fine here — the site is doing the selling.)*

### Beat 4 — RE-OPEN THE LOOP (the hybrid's whole edge — A's tension, re-armed AFTER proof)
> *(After they react — "huh, that's actually mine?")*
> "Right? So here's the honest part — what you're looking at, Alex built that from your Google listing and your reviews. It's real, it's yours to keep either way. But it's basically the rough cut. The good version is when Alex hops on for 15 minutes and swaps in YOUR actual photos, your real number, your colors, and points it at your own domain so it's *actually yours*, not on our subdomain. That part can't happen without you."

### Beat 5 — Price OUT LOUD, loss-reversed (the skeptics demand this before they'll move)
> "And before you ask — there's no deposit, nothing today. If you love it and want it on your domain, it's $450 one time. That's the founding price for our first ten businesses — you'd be one of them — then it goes to $700 for good. And here's the part that makes it easy: point it at your domain, run it 30 days. If you don't think it makes you the obvious choice over the other guy, I refund the $450 and you keep the site live anyway. Don't want it at all? You keep this draft, delete my number, you'll never get a bill you didn't say yes to."

> *(The guarantee lives on the $450 itself, not just the free draft — Hormozi: stack the risk-reversal where the money changes hands. After this line a "no" means "I'd rather look worse than my competitor for free-to-try," which is the insane-to-refuse position. Spoken at Beat 5 and mirrored in post-call E3, booking 1A, and PAY-1.)*

### Beat 6 — Book the call BEFORE they hang up to "think" (assumptive, no-oriented Voss close)
> "So that 15 minutes with Alex to make it actually yours and lock the founding price before it jumps — would it be a bad idea to grab it tomorrow morning, or is afternoon better?"
> *(Pin a same-day or next-day slot. Confirm the number. Then:)*
> "Done. Alex'll call you {slot}. And the link's already in your texts — keep poking at it, write down anything you want changed, that's exactly what tomorrow's for."

### Cold safety-net (ONLY if a lead slipped through un-built — no link to send)
Fall back to the existing COLD opener (`REVEAL-OPENERS.md` §2): future-tense gift ("I want to build {company} a website — done, live by this time tomorrow, free"), capture the number, and the **next-day "it's live, here's the link" text becomes a true SPEC-grade second touch** that re-enters this exact hybrid flow. Never run "it's live, want the link" on an un-built lead — that's the banned scam-flavored lie.

### Win / half-win definitions
- **Win:** link texted + reacted-to + 15-min personalization call booked at a pinned time + number confirmed.
- **Half-win:** link texted + reacted, no time pinned → default the call to same-time-tomorrow, say so, send the SMS+email re-touch sequence (recoverable — unlike A's dead booking).

## 5) THE ONE BIGGEST RISK — and how the script neutralizes it

**The winner's #1 risk = the post-preview GHOST** (B's inherited weakness): they pocket the free live draft, the dopamine loop closes, the curiosity that would've pulled them to the call is already satisfied, and they say "great, thanks, I'll think about it" — endowment cutting the wrong way.

**Five mechanisms in the script neutralize it — all fire BEFORE they can hang up to "think":**

1. **The link never lands naked.** It arrives *welded to an incompletion* — "it's the rough cut, the good version needs you." The thing they now own is explicitly **unfinished**, so possession is a Zeigarnik hook (open loop on a half-finished possession), not a satisfied curiosity. *(Beat 4)*
2. **The loss is made concrete and time-boxed.** They don't just "gain a draft," they stand to **lose the $450 founding price** (→$700) and the personalization. Loss aversion > the convenience of ghosting. *(Beat 5)*
3. **The domain is the un-substitutable next step.** The draft sits on `{company}.wediditforyou.com` — *useless to them* until it's on their own domain with their name, and **they literally cannot do that without Alex.** The free thing is deliberately incomplete in the one way that matters. *(Beat 4)*
4. **The booking happens before "I'll think about it" can form** — assumptively, while the dopamine is hot, on a logistics frame ("make it actually yours"), not an evaluative one ("should you buy"). The skeptics (Burned, Dale) are explicit: *text it and let me go = I ghost; text it and ride the proud-feeling straight into the booking = you get my card.* *(Beat 6)*
5. **No-show is recoverable anyway.** Unlike A (a missed booking = a dead sale, nothing real ever entered their world), the hybrid leaves the live link + a real SMS/email re-touch sequence in their hands — a missed slot is a *delay*, not a death. *(Half-win + §8 of CALL-SCRIPTS)*

> **The neutralization in one line:** Don't hand over the value at zero commitment — hand over *proof of the value* and keep the *completion* (your domain, your photos, your founding price) behind the booked call. They've seen enough to believe, and not enough to leave.
